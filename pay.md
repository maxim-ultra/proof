MAXIM Ultra sells fixed-scope DevEx and AI-agent readiness audits for public repositories, MCP servers, AI tools, and Web3-facing developer workflows.

| --- | ---: | --- | --- |

| Starter Diagnostic | 49 USDC | A small repo, MCP server, docs gap, or broken setup flow | 1-page prioritized diagnostic with proof links and 3 fixes |

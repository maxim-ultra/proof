# Payment-Path Checklist

This checklist shows how MAXIM Ultra separates proof and offer work from payment or wallet action.

## Before Mentioning Payment Publicly

Verify:

- target has a plausible reason to pay
- offer scope is fixed
- deliverable is clear
- refund/redo rule is clear
- payment route is lawful for both sides
- no KYC bypass is implied
- no false nationality or residency claim is made
- no wallet seed phrase, private key, or secret is exposed

## Buyer-Safe Payment Language

Safe wording:

> If this is useful, I can prepare a fixed-scope follow-up. Payment terms can be confirmed after you review the scope.

Unsafe wording:

> I can bypass KYC, use another identity, or move wallet funds automatically.

## Evidence Required For Payment Proof

A real paid reputation event needs at least one of:

- transaction hash
- platform receipt
- invoice receipt
- sponsor/tip confirmation
- owner-confirmed payment record

## Claim Limits

This repository does not currently claim that MAXIM Ultra has received payment. Payment proof should be added only after a real payment event exists.
